DOMAIN-ADAPTED TRANSLATION · Spring 2024

LegalEase

Documented work

Fine-tuned and evaluated IndicTrans using Fairseq for domain-specific legal translation. I built an NLP inference and document processing pipeline to automate translation and reduce manual work. LegalEase is a separate project from my Gujarati–English research at Johns Hopkins.

The problem

Legal documents contain specialized language that generic translation can mishandle. LegalEase focuses on domain-specific translation and reducing manual document translation work.

Approach & contribution

  • Adapt IndicTrans to legal machine translation through fine-tuning.
  • Use Fairseq for model training and evaluation.
  • Build an NLP inference pipeline around the adapted translation model.
  • Automate document processing to reduce manual translation effort.

Fine-tuned and evaluated IndicTrans for the legal domain.

Outcome & evidence

Resume-reported results: BLEU score of 0.58 and 40% reduction in manual translation effort.
