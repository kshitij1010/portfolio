Language without barriers

Documented work

At the Johns Hopkins Center for Language and Speech Processing, I researched Gujarati-to-English neural machine translation using LLM-assisted methods. I optimized MarianMT and NLLB through training and error analysis, with evaluation on Flores200.

The problem

Low-resource language pairs have limited training data. This research studies how LLM-assisted methods and specialized neural translation models can improve Gujarati-to-English translation.

Approach & contribution

  • Research LLM-assisted methods for Gujarati-to-English neural machine translation.
  • Implement and optimize MarianMT and NLLB training and evaluation workflows.
  • Evaluate translation performance using Flores200 as documented in the resumes.
  • Use error analysis to identify and reduce translation failures.

Conducted low-resource translation research as a Research Assistant at Johns Hopkins CLSP.

Outcome & evidence

Resume-reported results: 15% improvement on Flores200 and 20% reduction in translation errors.

The resumes report 15% improvement on Flores200 and 20% fewer translation errors. Exact baseline configurations, metric definitions, and evaluation split sizes are not supplied in the public resume.
